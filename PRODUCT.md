# CinéPop

## Platform
Web, running locally under XAMPP.

## Purpose and users
IE4727 Theme 5 cinema ticket booking portal. Customers browse films and schedules, shortlist multiple preferences, select available bookings and pay through a local test adapter. Administrators manage films, screenings, bookings and enquiries.

## Constraints
Preserve all current pages, copy, movie data, prices, user records and booking behaviour. The base uses HTML, custom CSS, vanilla JavaScript, PHP and MySQL. Acknowledgements go to the local Mercury mailbox and are read in Thunderbird. Real payment processing is outside this local project.

## Confirmed brand direction
User requested the design of https://www.odeon.co.uk/ on 30 October 2026, while keeping existing pages and content. Adopt its navy/blue cinema presentation and task-focused navigation; retain CinéPop branding and existing film artwork. Do not import ODEON templates, branding, commercial offers or external social links.
