# Site-wide redesign

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Make the existing CinéPop portal feel like a coherent commercial cinema website. Follow the user's ODEON reference while retaining all present content and workflows.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep navy regions, clear electric-blue actions, white booking controls, square poster crops, restrained borders, condensed display lettering and readable Nunito Sans body text. Replace the gold/serif/glowing visual system across all pages.

**STORY:** Discover a film through imagery, find its screening, choose seats, compare preferences and complete checkout. The display leads to usable booking controls rather than decorative panels.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Clean navy navigation and a white CinéPop wordmark above a large image-led film feature. The film title and primary booking action sit at the left; a fixed white quick-book bar anchors the homepage. Inner pages reuse the same typography, blue actions and clear forms/tables.

**FORM:** Code-led implementation of the user's pinned reference. No concept lottery applies to the already selected direction. seed: none (unavailable).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Content boundary
No film descriptions, prices, forms, pages or features are removed. Existing artwork is retained. Functional navigation and a homepage film/date quick-book form may be added. Existing user data is preserved. Reference inspection: live ODEON browser on 30 October 2026, desktop homepage and film section.
